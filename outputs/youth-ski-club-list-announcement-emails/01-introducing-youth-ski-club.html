Something new is happening on the water this fall — the Bell Acqua Lake Youth Ski Club.

It's competition-style coaching in all three water skiing events — slalom, trick, and jump — for skiers ages 10 to 17, on our own private, wind-protected lake. No crowds, no chop, just certified coaches and tournament boats running real sets, two afternoons a week, 4:00–6:30 PM (Tuesday/Thursday or Monday/Wednesday).

The season opens Tuesday, September 15 and runs through October 31 — 28 coached sessions in all.

What's included

• Certified coaches and tournament boats
• All ski gear — nothing to buy, nothing to haul
• Hot tub, warm showers, lockers, and WiFi at the dock
• Beginner through competitive — every skier trains at their own level

$2,000 for the full season, all-inclusive. Weekend lessons are available separately if your skier wants extra time on the water.

Every skier gets real time behind the boat, so we keep each cohort small by design — seats go to whoever registers first.
