## Email

**Subject:** Two new upgrades live — boat log + member calendar visibility

Hey Mike,

Two upgrades went live on the booking system this week.

**1. Members can now see the other calendars.**
Inside the member portal, every hour now shows what's happening on the lesson and ski ride side. If a lesson or ski ride is booked at the top of the hour, the member sees a gold marker that says "driver on water" — so they know a coach and boat driver will already be out there right before their slot. If nothing's scheduled, it says so. This came straight from the members who need a driver for their rides — now they can pick their times around when someone's already on the water.

**2. The boat log is live (the digital BA Hours sheet).**
There's a new page built for the iPad:

https://meek-duckanoo-53e84b.netlify.app/bell-acqua-boatlog.html

When someone finishes a ride, they open it, tap their name — it already lists everyone booked that day from all three calendars — and enter the boat, the engine hours at start and stop (it calculates the quantity automatically), the driver, and any notes. For rides that never hit a calendar, there's a manual entry button where they type in the name, date, time, boat, and hours by hand. Everything lands in one log that looks like the paper sheet — date, name, guest, boat, start, stop, qty, driver, notes — with running hour totals per boat, and it exports to a spreadsheet with one tap. Entries can be corrected but not deleted, so the record stays honest. There's also a Boat Log button in the staff dashboard.

Easiest way to start: open that link on the iPad, leave it up at the dock, and have people log their ride on the way off the water.
