# How to use the Bell Acqua Lake app

The app has two parts:

- **Member Portal** is where you book your ski times: https://meek-duckanoo-53e84b.netlify.app/bell-acqua-member.html
- **Boat Log** is where you record the boat hours after every ride: https://meek-duckanoo-53e84b.netlify.app/bell-acqua-boatlog.html

Tip: bookmark both on your phone. The Boat Log also has a QR code posted at the dock.

---

## 1. Logging in to the Member Portal

1. Open the Member Portal link above.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**. This is the email you signed up with and the portal password you created at checkout (or the temporary password we gave you).
3. Tap **Sign In**.
4. You'll see your name at the top and your membership dates (for example "Active: 5/1/2026 – 4/30/2027").

Good to know:

- **Forgot your password?** The app doesn't have a reset button yet, so text Mike and we'll reset it for you.
- **Want a new password?** Once you're signed in, tap **⚙️ Settings**, type a new password (at least 6 characters), and tap **Save Profile**. You can update your name, phone and address there too.
- **Seeing "Booking locked"?** Every skier on your membership has to sign their waiver before booking opens. Check your email for the waiver links. The portal unlocks by itself once the last waiver is signed.
- **Using a shared tablet?** The portal keeps you signed in on that device, so tap **Sign Out** when you're done.

## 2. Booking a ski time

Member times are the **bottom half of each hour**, in 15-minute slots (for example 8:30–8:45 and 8:45–9:00), from 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM.

1. Under **Select a Date**, use the ‹ › arrows to find the month, then tap a day. Grayed-out days can't be booked. That includes past days, days outside your membership dates, and days staff have closed. **Midweek members** can only book Monday–Thursday.
2. The **Available Slots** panel lists every hour. Tap a white slot to select it. It changes to **Selected**.
3. Pick **1 or 2 slots** for the day. **Two is the daily maximum.** They can be back to back or hours apart, whatever suits you.
4. Tap the green **Confirm 2 Booking(s)** button.
5. You'll see "Bookings confirmed!" Your slots turn green with **✓ your name** and appear under **My Upcoming Bookings**.

**What the slots mean**

- **White:** open, so tap to select it.
- **Green ✓ your name:** your booking.
- **Gray with a first name:** another member has it. Want to ski together? Book next to them.
- **Unavailable:** blocked by staff.
- **Limit or ±2hr:** you've hit the 2-slot limit, or the slot is more than 2 hours from your other one.

**See when a driver is already on the water**

Next to each hour you'll see the lesson and ski-ride calendar for the **top of that hour**:

- **🚤 Gold, "Lesson 9:00–9:30 — driver on water"**: a lesson or ski ride is booked, so a coach and boat driver will already be out right before your member slots. This is the best time to book if you need a driver.
- **Gray, "no lesson / ski ride booked"**: nothing is scheduled that hour yet.

## 3. Changing or cancelling a booking

The portal doesn't have a "move" button. To change a time, cancel it and book the new one.

**To cancel:**

1. Under **My Upcoming Bookings**, tap **Cancel** next to the booking.
   *or* open that day on the calendar and tap your green **✓** slot.
2. Tap **OK** on "Cancel this booking?"
3. You'll see "Booking cancelled", and the slot opens up for other members.

**To change:** cancel the old slot(s) first, then follow the booking steps above to pick the new time.

Please cancel **before midnight the day before** your ride. Your membership agreement says late cancellations are charged for the ski ride.

## 4. Logging your ride in the Boat Log (after you ski)

Log **every ride, every time**, before you leave the dock. Use the dock iPad or scan the QR code with your phone. You don't need to log in.

**If your ride was booked on the calendar**

1. Open the Boat Log. It opens on **today's date**.
2. Under "Finished your ride? Tap your name to log the boat hours," find your name card. You can type in **🔍 Find your name…** to search.
3. Tap your card. The **Log Boat Hours** form opens with your name, the date, your booked time and the activity already filled in.
4. Choose the **Boat** (03 Ski Nautique, Red 21 Pro Star, or **Other…**).
5. Read the boat's engine-hour meter (odometer). Enter **Engine Hours — Start** and **Engine Hours — Stop**. **Hours Used** fills in by itself.
6. Type the **Coach / Driver** name (required). Names used before will pop up as suggestions.
7. Add a **Guest** name or **Notes** if needed. Both are optional.
8. Tap **Save Entry**. You'll see "Saved — thank you! 🚤" and your card turns **green: ✓ Logged**.

**Walk-ons and rides that aren't on the calendar**

1. Tap **＋ Manual Entry** at the top right.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Date**, **Time**, **Activity** (Member Ride, B/I Lesson, Ski Ride, Fuel, Maintenance, Other), **Boat**, the **Start / Stop** engine hours, and **Coach / Driver**.
3. Tap **Save Entry**.

**Made a mistake?**

- Tap your green card, or tap your line in the **📒 Log Entries** table. The form opens as **Edit Log Entry**. Fix it and tap **Save Entry**.
- Entries **can't be deleted** from the app. If something needs to be removed, tell Mike.

**Other handy bits**

- Use the **date box** at the top to see or log a different day.
- **Log Entries** shows the day's rides with hour totals per boat. **Recent (all)** shows earlier days too, and **⬇ Export CSV** downloads it as a spreadsheet.

## 5. At the dock: keeping the Boat Log up on the iPad / tablet

**One-time setup**

1. On the iPad, open the Boat Log link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. On an Android tablet in Chrome, tap **⋮** and then **Add to Home screen**.
3. Keep the screen from going to sleep. On an iPad, go to **Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never**. On Android, go to **Settings → Display → Screen timeout** and pick the longest option.
4. Keep the tablet **plugged in**. The screen will be on all day.
5. *Optional:* to stop people wandering off to other apps, turn on **Guided Access** (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access). Then triple-click the side or home button while the Boat Log is open.

**Every day**

- **Refresh the page each morning.** The Boat Log loads the day's rides when it opens and stays on that date, so a tablet left on overnight will still show yesterday.
- If someone booked after the page was opened and their card isn't showing, refresh the page, or use **＋ Manual Entry**.
- Leave the Boat Log on screen. If anyone uses the tablet for the Member Portal, remind them to **Sign Out**.
