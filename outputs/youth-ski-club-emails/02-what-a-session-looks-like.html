Most parents want to picture the afternoon before they commit. Here's how a Youth Ski Club session runs.

4:00pm — Arrive and gear up

Your skier changes at the dock. Lockers and changing rooms are on site, and every ski, vest, and rope they need is already here — nothing to buy, nothing to haul.

4:15pm — On the boat

Bell Acqua is a private, wind-protected lake built for training, so the water is glass when the public lakes are chop. Skiers run sets in small rotations behind tournament boats with a certified coach calling every pass.

Through 6:30pm — Three events, real progress

Club is 3-event waterskiing: slalom, trick, and jump. Beginners start with the basics of a solid slalom pass. More advanced skiers work the course, refine trick runs, and build toward the jump. Everyone trains at their own level in the same group.

After the last set

Hot tub, warm showers, WiFi at the dock, and a refrigerator for snacks — so pickup isn't a shivering kid in a wet towel.

Four of these a week, for the whole fall season.
