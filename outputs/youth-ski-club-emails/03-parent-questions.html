These are the five things parents ask us before their skier's first session.

“My kid has never been on skis.”

Perfect. Club is built for all skill levels, ages 10–17. Beginners get coached from the first deep-water start — they're not thrown in with the competitive kids and left behind.

“Do we need to buy gear?”

No. Skis, vests, ropes, tournament boats — all included in the season price. No boat to tow, no equipment to outgrow.

“Is it safe?”

Every session is run by certified coaches on a private lake with no public traffic, in tournament boats designed for exactly this. Skiers are never on the water unsupervised.

“What if they can only make some of the sessions?”

Four sessions a week is what's available — not a mandatory attendance quota. Pick the schedule that fits: Tuesday & Thursday, or Monday & Wednesday, 4–6:30pm. Come to what you can.

“What about weekends?”

Weekend lessons are available for an additional fee if your skier wants more time on the water.
