## Part 1 — Regeneration (Win-Back) Campaign Ideas

The roadmap already segments the database into three groups. Below are implementable win-back concepts for each, plus two new angles — pick whichever fit the calendar alongside the new-customer campaign in Part 2.

### 1. "We Miss You on the Water" — Dormant Client Reactivation
- **Audience:** Anyone with no booking in 90+ days (pull from GHL/Supabase by last-booking date)
- **Offer:** 20% off their next lesson or ski ride (matches the existing historical-client discount already defined in the ICP)
- **Channel:** Email + SMS, 3-touch sequence over 10 days
- **Hook:** "It's been a while — the lake's still here, and so is your 20% off."

### 2. Old Non-Ski-Ride Clients → Beginner/Intermediate 2-for-1
- **Audience:** Past lesson clients who never upgraded to a ski ride
- **Offer:** 2-for-1 beginner/intermediate lesson (already scoped in the ICP as a target segment, not yet executed as its own send)
- **Channel:** Segmented email/SMS blast
- **Hook:** Position as a "level-up" — they already know the basics, this is the nudge to go further with a friend.

### 3. VIP / Database Re-Engagement Round 2
- **Audience:** The VIP/new-database list from the June giveaway (leads who entered but didn't convert)
- **Offer:** Same 2-for-1 VIP structure, refreshed creative — "Your VIP status never expired"
- **Channel:** Email + SMS
- **Note:** This list already opted in once; lowest lift of the three, run it first if you need a quick win.

### 4. Bring-a-Friend-Back Referral (new idea)
- **Audience:** Lapsed clients (90+ days), same pool as #1
- **Offer:** "Come back with a friend, you both get $50 off" — turns a win-back into a 2-for-1 lead gen play at the same time
- **Why it works:** Regeneration + referral in one send; doubles the reactivation's lead value.

### 5. "Lock In Your 2026 Rate" Loyalty Push (new idea)
- **Audience:** Members and repeat clients with renewals inside the Aug–Oct window
- **Offer:** No discount needed — creative angle only, positions renewal as protecting against a future price increase
- **Why it works:** Complements the FIN renewal-reminder track already running (Weeks 2, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) with a marketing hook instead of a bare invoice reminder.

**Recommendation:** Run #3 (VIP re-engagement) in parallel with the new-customer campaign below since it's the lowest-lift — the list already opted in. Sequence #1, #2, #4, #5 into September/October once the new-customer engine (Part 2) is live and the team isn't split across two builds at once.

## Part 2 — Featured Campaign: "Buy 2, Get 1 Lesson FREE" (New Customer Lead Gen)

This is the campaign Mike raised — built out as a full lead-gen play for **brand-new, never-booked customers**, distinct from the existing `B2G1JULY2026` ski-ride offer (which targets the existing database as a retention play). Same mechanic, new audience, new purpose: **lower the first-lesson barrier to feed the top of the funnel.**

### Why this offer specifically
- The roadmap's Week 1 ad budget already earmarks **$100 for "B2G1 Free for first-timers"** — this campaign is that budget line, fully built out.
- Lessons are priced at **$75/15-min slot, 2-slot minimum ($150)** on the live booking system. A Buy-2-Get-1 structure maps perfectly: book 2 slots ($150), 3rd slot ($75 value) free — no new pricing logic needed, mirrors the exact mechanic already proven with ski rides.
- It directly feeds the funnel the roadmap is built around: **Lead → Lesson Booking → Repeat → Member**, where 27.5% of current paying members came from a lesson. Every free-3rd-slot booking is a shot at that 27.5%.
- Lowers the friction that a first-timer feels most: paying full price without knowing if they'll like it. "Try it 3x for the price of 2" is a much easier yes than a single $75 slot.

### Offer Mechanics
- **Offer:** Buy 2 lesson slots ($150), get a 3rd slot free ($75 value) — new customers only, first booking.
- **Promo code:** `B2G1LESSON` (new, distinct from `B2G1JULY2026` so reporting doesn't mix new-lead performance with existing-database win-back performance).
- **Eligibility:** First-time bookers only — add a simple flag/note at checkout or in the GHL workflow to prevent existing clients from double-dipping into a new-customer-only budget.
- **Expiration:** Recommend a rolling 14-day window from ad click to booking, refreshed monthly, rather than a hard calendar cutoff — keeps it evergreen as the standing new-customer offer for the quarter.

### Target Audience
Reuse the roadmap's already-winning setup rather than starting cold:
- **Primary:** The proven Soccer-interest / affluent / frequent-traveler Meta audience (~$1.81 CPL)
- **Test audience:** Boating/lake-life interest, lookalike built from the member list — the roadmap's Week 1 alternate angle (boat owners, lake life, water sport activities, wakeboarding, water skiing, wake surfing, inner tubing)

### Funnel
1. FB/IG Lead ad (Lead objective) → lead form captures Name, Email, Phone, "How did you hear about us?" (pulls forward the Week 7 tracking fix so it's live from day one of this campaign, not week 7)
2. GHL auto-response confirms the offer + promo code, links to the lesson booking page
3. Client books 2 slots ($150) → 3rd slot auto-applied free via promo code at checkout
4. Lesson delivered (OPS)
5. Automated post-lesson membership follow-up sequence (email + SMS) triggers — same automation already scheduled for Week 2 of the roadmap

### Ad Creative Angles
- **Angle A — Try It Risk-Reduced:** "Never skied before? Try 3 lessons for the price of 2 — see why 1 in 4 of our members started exactly here." (ties the offer directly to the 27.5% proof point, which is a strong, honest hook)
- **Angle B — Aerial Exclusivity:** Reuse the Week 1 aerial "private lake" creative with the B2G1 offer overlaid — pairs the roadmap's existing exclusivity creative with this specific offer.
- **Angle C — Testimonial:** A past first-timer's story ("I'd never skied before this summer...") paired with the offer as the CTA.

Sample primary text (Angle A):
> Never been on skis before? Bell Acqua Lake is a private, uncrowded lake with your own driver and coach — no crowds, no wake, no pressure. Right now, first-time students get 3 lessons for the price of 2. Book your first session today.
> 👉 Use code B2G1LESSON at checkout.

### Budget
- Sits inside the existing **$1,000/mo roadmap budget**, specifically the **$100 "giveaway/urgency offers" line** already allocated in Week 1 — no new spend required to launch.
- If early CPL/booking-rate data looks strong, recommend pulling incremental budget from the **$200 testing line** rather than the **$700 core line**, to protect the already-proven audience while this scales.

### KPIs to Track (campaign-specific, layers on top of the roadmap's KPI scoreboard)
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead (this campaign) | ≤ $3 |
| Lead → booked-2-slots rate | Track as new baseline (no prior data) |
| 3rd-slot redemption rate | Track — signals real offer usage vs. leads who book but don't redeem |
| Lesson → membership conversion | Benchmark against the existing 27.5% roadmap figure |
